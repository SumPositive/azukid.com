Getting started

1Administrator invitation

An administrator sets the person's name and role, then provides a QR code or a ten-minute invitation code

2Register the device

Scan the QR code with the standard camera or enter the code, then register a passkey using device biometrics

3Sign in with a passkey

No password is used. Sign in with the device's face, fingerprint, or screen lock verification

4Start from Today

After sign-in, the app opens the work items that need your attention now

Names are managed centrally

Users do not choose their own nicknames. An administrator assigns and changes display names

The Today screen

Below the date, the screen shows only the work where you are included as an assignee

To-dosShows three assigned open to-dos in priority order and always includes every item due today
SalesSales and management staff see their active assigned opportunities
Job sitesEmployees can see all sites. Partner contractors see only their assigned sites
CollectionsManagement staff see assigned receivables, invoices, and payment progress
Current statusThe top right shows gray Not clocked in or blue Working. Contractors see Not entered / On site
Issue reportedAn issue is highlighted separately from workflow status and shown to its selected employee audience

Employees can use View all in each list to inspect the overall picture. Partner contractors never receive data for unassigned sites

Business workflow

1Sales

Move from inquiry to meeting, estimate, and won while recording billing details during the sales stage

2Convert to a job site

Carry a won opportunity into a site with address, cautions, effort, period, contact, and assignees

3Perform the work

Create assigned to-dos and share communication, handovers, and the latest issue status within the site

4Collections

Move from receivable to invoice and paid while managing transfer or collection dates

Communication

NO ONE-TO-ONE DIRECT MESSAGES
Three or more peopleEvery communication includes an administrator and is shared by a team, never just two people
Nominate respondentsChoose one or more required respondents. Each nominated person answers OK or NG
Optional noteAdd text only when an OK or NG response needs context
Action, unread, allFilter the list around what you need to handle first
Changes and historyChanging the message records the before-and-after text and shows its history to every viewer

The author or an administrator can change the message body. Participants, required respondents, reads, and responses stay fixed. Change history appears only when a change exists

Attendance

Contractors see Entry / Exit instead of Clock in / Clock out. Executives record attendance by tapping the Today button, without choosing a location

Where a punch terminal is availableShow your one-time QR code to a registered terminal. Clock in or Clock out is determined automatically from your previous record
Where no terminal is availableChoose an assigned sale, assigned site, or Other, then tap Punch. Other requires a location name
1Open Attendance

Tap Not clocked in or Working at the top right of Today. Contractors see Not entered / On site

2Choose a method

Show the QR code to a terminal or select a location for direct punching

3Complete the punch

After a direct punch succeeds, the app returns to Today and shows the result

4Review records

The Attendance screen shows your current status and today's records

A punch terminal opens its scanning screen automatically after sign-in. On success it sounds a cue and shows a large blue Clock in or green Clock out result for three seconds

Visibility by role

Executive / Works ManagerReviews the organization, administration, audit, and change history
Administration / SafetyInvites users, manages sales, sites and collections, and corrects attendance
Sales / Site SupervisorManages opportunities, sees all sites, and advances assigned work
In-house TechnicianSees all sites and handles assigned work and communication
Partner ContractorSees only assigned sites and the work information required at those sites
Punch TerminalScans QR codes and records punches without displaying business information

Administrator operations

Invite usersSet the person's name and role, then provide the registration QR or code in person
Activate or stop accessStopping a former worker or contractor also returns their open screens to sign-in
Correct attendanceKeep the original time when correcting a record and export monthly CSV data
Audit and historyReview the operations that matter, such as sensitive views and state changes

Sample-only person switching

In the sample, tap the name in the header to list fictional users by role and switch to the selected person's view

Not an operational feature

You can switch to any active user except the person currently shown and punch terminals. The previous person appears after switching, the session is not treated as passkey-authenticated, and every switch is audited

Safe usage principles

No business data stored on the devicePages are not cached, so business information is not retained after sign-out
No passwordsPasskeys and device verification replace passwords that are easily shared or reused
Do not fetch unassigned dataUnauthorized data is rejected by the API, not merely hidden in the screen
Do not overwrite instructionsInstructions and their changes remain available for later review
