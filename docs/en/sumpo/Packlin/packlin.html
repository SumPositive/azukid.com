Packlin User Guide

Item list and packing preparation app

List belongings for trips, business travel, events, and hobby gear, then prepare your packing while checking quantities and weight.

For each pack, choose Tags to sort items freely, or Groups to organize them in layers.

Use checklists, sharing, backup, and AI-assisted suggestions to reduce forgotten items and make preparation easier.

Basic Flow

1 Create a Pack

Create a pack for each trip, event, or group of items you want to manage.

2 Add Items

Enter required quantity, stock quantity, and weight, and sort items with tags.

3 Check Progress

Review what is ready, what is missing, and how much the pack weighs.

4 Share or Save

Export packs or create backups when you need to share or preserve data.

⚠️ Data is not encrypted. Do not store personal or confidential information.

About Undo

The goal is to minimize extra steps. There are no confirmation prompts and no Save button after editing. "Delete" removes items immediately without confirmation.

If you make a mistake, tap "Undo ↩︎" to revert to the previous state — up to 10 steps back. Redo lets you step forward again.

Organize By (Tags / Groups)

You can choose how each pack is organized. Tap the bag icon in the pack list to open Edit pack, and switch with Organize by. New packs start with Tags.

Two ways

Tags Items sit in one flat list, and you sort them loosely by attaching tags. One item can have several tags. Opening the pack takes you straight to Item browse. In the pack list, the pack icon shows a tag hanging from the bag's handle.
Groups Sort strictly in three layers: Pack (bag), Group (pouch), and Item. Each item always belongs to exactly one group.
Switching You can switch at any time, and no items or tags are lost. When you switch from Groups to Tags, each group name is attached as a tag to the items in that group.
Tag-mode group Items added with Tags go into the "Tag-mode group". When you switch back to Groups, you'll find them in this group.

Tags

Add / remove In the Tags section of the item detail, tap a tag to attach or remove it. Type in the field above to find a tag; if it isn't there, tap "Add" to attach it as a new tag.
Rename / delete Press and hold a tag to rename it, and items in this pack with the same tag change too. While renaming, the trash button removes that tag from every item in this pack.
In lists Set "Details" in Settings to With tags or With tags & memo to show tags on each row.

Item Browse

Item browse shows every item in a pack in one list, across all groups. With Tags, opening a pack takes you to this screen. With Groups, open it from the "Item Browse" button at the bottom of the group list. Handy for a final check before departure or for reviewing your load.

Sorting

Unchecked Brings unchecked items to the top. Ideal for a final check before departure.
Shortage count Lists items whose stock is below the required quantity, largest shortage first.
Shortage weight Lists items by the weight of the shortfall (shortage × unit weight), largest first.
Stock weight Lists items by current stock weight (stock × unit weight), largest first. Useful when your bag is overweight.
Ascending / descending The button to the right of the sort option switches between ascending and descending order.
Re-sort Rows don't move right away when you check items or change counts. When the order is out of date, the sort option turns gray; tap it to sort again.

Filtering and search

Tag list Choose a tag to show only items with that tag. "No tag" is also available. Drag the handle below the list up or down to change its height.
Search Narrows the list as you type, matching tags and item names and memos.
Total weight (Tags) The end of the list shows the total weight of the items shown. When filtered by a tag, it's the total for that tag.

Actions

Item detail Tap a row to open the item detail.
Quantity panel Tap the weight and count on a row to open a callout where you can change the weight per item, stock, and required count on the spot. It also shows the memo and tags.
Add (Tags) Add items with the ＋ button at the top right, or with "Add to end" at the end of the list.
Bulk edit (Tags) Tap "Bulk edit", select rows by tapping them, then use "Actions" to Add tags, Remove tags, Copy to another pack, or Delete all at once. "Select all shown" selects the filtered results in one go.

Quantity Input

Enter the weight per item, stock, and required count in the quantity section of the item detail, or in the quantity panel of a list.

Input methods

Stepper Adjust by 1 with the ＋ and − buttons (default).
Dial Drag the dial left or right to adjust the value. The button at the top right of the quantity section in the item detail switches between the stepper and the dial.
Numeric keypad Tap a number to open a keypad at the bottom of the screen and type the value directly. It can also calculate with ÷ × − ＋; fractions from division are rounded to the nearest whole number.

Dial settings

How to open When the dial is shown, open "Dial" settings at the top right of the quantity section.
Style Choose from 9 styles: Legacy / Midnight / Brass / Ocean / Shape / Varnia / Chrome / Hairline / Rubber.
Sensitivity preset Choose from 5 levels: Fine / Mild / Standard / Light / Fast.
Fine-tuning Adjust pitch, smoothing, and inertia start speed with sliders.

Pack Sharing & Backup

Export

Single pack Choose "Export" from the pack menu to export one pack as a .packlin file. Share it via AirDrop, email, Notes, or any other method.
All packs (backup) Use "Export All Packs (Backup)" in Settings to save all packs as a single .packlin file. Useful for device migration and data safety.

Import

How to import Tap "Load Pack" in Settings and select a file. Both single-pack and backup files are imported the same way.
Duplicate handling When restoring a backup, packs with matching IDs are overwritten. For single-pack imports, packs with the same name are overwritten. Non-matching packs are added as new.
File detection The app detects whether a file is a single pack or a backup by reading the JSON content, not the file extension.

Pack Publishing

Save the packs you create to the cloud and publish them. Published packs can be viewed and imported by other users, so everyone can share handy packing lists. You can also import packs published by other users as your own.

⚠️ Published pack contents are visible to anyone. Do not include personal or confidential information.

Publishing

How to publish Choose "Publish" from the pack edit menu to save the pack to the cloud and make it public. You can set an author nickname to display (if left blank, it is published as "Anonymous").
Re-publish / Unpublish Publishing the same pack again overwrites its contents. You can unpublish (delete) a pack from the published packs list.
Published indicator Packs that are currently published show a "Public" badge below the bag icon in the list.

Importing

How to import Open the published packs list from the pack list screen and import any pack you like as your own. Sorting by popular / recent and keyword search are supported.
Share link Open a pack in the published packs list to see its "Public pack share link". Tap "Copy" to copy the link (URL), then paste it into an email or social post. When the recipient taps the link, the public pack is imported into Packlin.
About ads Importing published packs asks you to watch a rewarded ad every so often (to support the service).

iCloud Backup

Your AI tickets and pack publishing ID are automatically backed up to iCloud. With iCloud enabled, your tickets and published status are restored when you switch devices. No sign-in or personal information is required.

AI Generation (Chappy)Optional

Tell Chappy where you're going or what the occasion is, and the AI will suggest a complete pack for you. The suggested pack is saved directly and can be edited freely afterward. You can also hand an existing pack to Chappy and ask for revisions.

You can send a request by watching a video ad. AI tickets are no longer sold as of 3.5.0. Tickets you already have can still be used (1 ticket per generation).

How to use

Create new Tap the ✦ button in the top-right of the pack list and choose "Ask Chappy to create one."
Revise existing Tap the ✦ button in the pack edit screen and choose "Ask Chappy to revise this." The current pack contents are passed to the AI for additions or edits.
Request text Up to 1,000 characters. Describe the destination, duration, purpose, season, or anything else that helps.

How to send a request

AI tickets Use the AI tickets you have. The number left is shown on the Chappy screen. Tickets are no longer sold.
Watch an ad When you have no tickets, watch a video ad from the Chappy screen to send a request without using a ticket.

Settings

Display

Display mode Beginner: shows operation hints in the header. Expert: hides hints for a compact layout.
Appearance System: follows the device setting. Light: always light mode. Dark: always dark mode.
Row detail Chooses what each row in a list shows. The first line always shows the name, weight, and count. With tags & memo: tags on line 2, memo on line 3. With tags: tags on line 2. Name (weight, count): first line only. Min: first line only, with tighter spacing. Tiny: even tighter. Lines with no tags or memo are not shown. Group rows show the memo only with With tags & memo.
Text size Easy-on-the-eyes! Auto: follows the system Dynamic Type setting. Or choose Standard / Large / Extra Large.

Behavior

New item position Choose whether new items, groups, and packs are added at the top or bottom of the list.

Weight

Show required weight Displays the weight for the required quantity (required × unit weight).
Show kg from 1,000 g When a total weight or an item's weight per item reaches 1,000 g, it is shown in "1.0 kg" format. Input is always in g.

Check & stock linking

Check ON fills stock When you check an item, the stock quantity is automatically raised to meet the required quantity.
Check OFF clears stock When you uncheck an item, the stock quantity is reset to 0.
